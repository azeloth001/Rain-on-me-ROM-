## Known conclusions

- Council confidence measures engine agreement; it is not automatically a calibrated CLEAN probability.
- Strict clean passage is rare. Practical utility can be higher because profitable round trips deserve partial credit without being mislabeled CLEAN.
- Short memory at 15% was safe and mildly interesting in G1, but not proven strongly enough for production.
- The original 8% continuous PHI-permeability formula was broadly harmful.
- PHI information is better researched as event semantics and post-touch behavior than as deformation of symmetric geometry.
- Real-clock horizons reduce the timeframe bias created by fixed bar horizons.
- Production and research must remain separate because research dashboards and duplicated MTF logic can exceed Pine's compiled-token limit.
