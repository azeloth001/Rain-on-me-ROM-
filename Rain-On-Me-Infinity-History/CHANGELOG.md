# Changelog

This changelog describes research intent and observed outcomes. It is not a promise that every archived script compiled or behaved identically on every TradingView environment.

## V3.0 foundation

### V3.0a — PHI Foundation

- Rebuilt the project in Pine v6.
- Added confirmed previous-period anchors.
- Preserved ROM Legacy ratios while adding Canonical and Hybrid families.
- Established 21 persistent PHI levels, ATR zones and bar-close events.
- Repaired the Ehlers-style cycle and replaced unsafe pressure logic.

### V3.0b / V3.0b1 — Resonance Field

- Added continuous Gaussian PHI affinity and MTF resonance.
- V3.0b1 restored visual richness after the first V3.0b pass felt weaker than the intended ROM identity.

### V3.0c — PHI Signal Council

- Introduced four independent families: Cycle, Flow, ATR and PHI Geometry.
- Added agreement, confidence, cooldown and confirmed Council events.

### V3.0d / V3.0d1 — Research, Beauty and Dashboard Intelligence

- Expanded Aurora/Ocean visual language, evidence glow and research telemetry.
- Turned the dashboard from a summary panel into a decision and diagnostic instrument.

## V3.1 evidence calibration

### V3.1a — Evidence Calibration

- Added independent prospective PHI reaction studies.
- Introduced MFE/MAE in ATR, Trend/Chop cohorts, Bayesian shrinkage and Wilson bounds.

### V3.1b — Calibration Integrity

- Strengthened event spacing, maturity gates, recency weighting and bar-close integrity.

## V3.2 router and forecast science

### V3.2a — Adaptive Edge Router

- Added evidence-driven `FOLLOW / FADE / WAIT / LEARN` routes.

### V3.2b — Router Auditor

- Froze prospective router decisions and measured their later outcomes.

### V3.2c — Outcome Integrity

- Corrected outcome bookkeeping and separated useful from misleading results.

### V3.2d — Ordered First Passage

- Classified outcomes by which barrier was reached first.
- Prevented favorable terminal closes from hiding earlier adverse failure.

### V3.2e — Forecast Skill

- Added Brier calibration, frozen climatology comparison and multiclass forecasts.
- Revealed that a 50% CLEAN prior was unrealistic when actual CLEAN rates were about 12–18%.

### V3.2e1 — Time-Safe Drawings

- Replaced long-anchor `bar_index` geometry with `xloc.bar_time`.
- Fixed the 1m historical-buffer crash caused by weekly anchors spanning up to 10,080 minute bars.

### V3.2f — Hierarchical Calibration

- Replaced the fixed CLEAN prior with learned empirical climatology.
- Shrunk FOLLOW/FADE forecasts toward genuine base rates.
- Froze adaptive confidence bands at event birth.
- Added strict CLEAN and separate practical utility (`UT`).
- Became the golden production/control branch.

### V3.2g / V3.2g1 — Adaptive and Factorial Labs

- Added Welford moments, recent-action memory, cycle telemetry and a PHI-permeability challenger.
- Split the experiment into Control, Memory-only, PHI-only and Combined arms on identical events.
- Retained the laboratory design.
- Rejected the current 8% continuous PHI term and Combined arm.

## H-series PHI research

### H1 — Structural PHI

- Replaced permeability with confirmed `REJECT / ACCEPT / RETEST / TOUCH` semantics.
- Capped research-only probability influence at 2%.

### H2 — Interaction-State Morphology

- Tested reclaim, probe, thrust, expansion and approach information known at event close.
- Retired after its diagnostic run.

### H3 / H4 — Density and Adaptive Salience

- H3 fixed-density gating failed.
- H4 tested adaptive salience; it did not consistently improve frozen forecasts.
- D/W tests still showed weekly geometry was visually meaningful.

### H5 family — Reaction behavior

- Reframed the question from direction alone to post-touch `HOLD / PASS / WHIP / STALL` behavior.
- Added retention, NEXT progression, dwell and close-side crossings.
- Excluded birth-candle self-scoring and separated overlapping raw events from horizon-separated evidence.

### H5f — Real-clock reaction families

- Formalized Immediate, Tactical and Structural clocks.
- Demonstrated that real-time horizons such as 1h/3h/6h are more meaningful than fixed bar counts across timeframes.

### H5g / H5g1 — Personality and compiled-limit split

- Added level personality: `WALL / GATE / MAGNET / MIXED / WEAK / EARLY`.
- H5g exceeded Pine's 100,256 compiled-token ceiling at 101,959 tokens.
- H5g1 became a leaner research artifact, reinforcing the need to separate production from research.

### FAIR and PV packs

- Split MICRO, INTRADAY and MACRO geometry/horizon experiments.
