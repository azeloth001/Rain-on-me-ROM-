# Architecture

## Production reasoning stack

### PHI geometry

- Confirmed primary and secondary higher-timeframe anchors.
- ROM Legacy, Canonical and Hybrid ratio families.
- Symmetric geometric affinity based on ATR-normalized distance.
- Interaction states: Approach, Touch, Reject, Accept and Retest.
- MTF confluence beam and evidence-weighted level glow.
- Timestamp coordinates for long-anchor safety.

### Four-family Council

| Family | Role |
|---|---|
| Cycle | Dominant-cycle trend and phase structure |
| Flow | Detrended pressure, normalized participation and guide behavior |
| ATR | Volatility-adjusted trail and structural direction |
| PHI Geometry | Lattice side, interaction memory and resonance |

The Council combines independent families with configurable weighting, minimum agreement, confidence gates and cooldown.

### Reaction research

Confirmed PHI events start non-overlapping prospective studies. Each study freezes context at birth and later records favorable/adverse excursion and response in ATR units.

Evidence includes:

- lifetime and recency-aware statistics;
- Trend/Chop cohorts;
- Bayesian neutral priors;
- Wilson confidence bounds;
- maturity stages such as EARLY and TRUSTED.

### Edge router

Completed evidence can recommend:

- `FOLLOW`
- `FADE`
- `WAIT`
- `LEARN`

The router remains Advisory by default. Confirm Council mode is deliberately stricter.

### Prospective calibration

At event birth, the auditor freezes probabilities and later scores ordered outcomes:

- CLEAN
- profitable or damaging ROUND TRIP
- LOSS
- WHIPSAW
- QUIET

Strict CLEAN probability and practical utility remain separate. Brier score compares forecasts with a frozen empirical baseline.

## Production/research boundary

V3.2f is the frozen production/control implementation. G/H-series scripts are research instruments. A challenger must never alter production routes, signals, drawings or candle colors until it has earned promotion on matched prospective evidence.

## MTF integrity

- Confirmed higher-timeframe values use completed HTF candles.
- Developing values may be shown visually but must not masquerade as confirmed evidence.
- Lower-than-chart timeframe rows should be unavailable rather than misleading.
- Long-anchor drawings use time coordinates, not distant historical bar indices.
